2. Summary

  • You need no account, no email address and no phone number. On first launch the app signs you in automatically with a random, anonymous ID.
  • We only process what the game needs: your nickname, the situations you type, the game history and – if you use selfie proof – your photos.
  • Rounds with all texts and photos are deleted automatically 7 days after the round ends (if a round never ends: 7 days after the last activity).
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics tools, no sale of data.
  • Data is stored by our provider Supabase on servers in Frankfurt am Main, Germany. Push notifications are sent via the Expo push service (USA).
  • The app uses the camera to scan QR codes and – only if you choose it – for selfie proof. Only your round sees your selfies – unless you share one yourself on the result image.

3. What data we process

3.1 Anonymous sign-in

DataRandom user ID (UUID), session token, time of sign-in; for technical reasons your IP address when connecting (see 3.10).
PurposeRecognising you in rounds without an account; preventing misuse.
Legal basisArt. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the app features you request).
RetentionAs long as you use the app. You can delete it at any time via "Delete my data" (see 7). IDs that have not been used for 12 months are deleted automatically.

3.2 Nickname

DataThe nickname (2–20 characters) you enter when joining a round. You don't have to use your real name.
PurposeShown to the other players in the round.
Legal basisArt. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
RetentionWith the round (7 days). If you block someone, their nickname stays in your block list until you unblock them or delete your data.

3.3 Rounds, situations and game history

DataRound data (name, occasion, game mode, card size, end time, round code, host; for rounds planned in advance – at most 5 days – also the party day, optional time and time zone); your situations (text up to 120 characters, optional emoji, category); your bingo card; marks (square, time, witness or selfie, optional "star", confirmed/rejected); game events, points, titles.
PurposeRunning the game: dealing cards, witness requests, selfie proof, scoring and award ceremony.
Who sees it?All players in the same round see your nickname, the situations on their cards, your confirmed marks and points. Nobody outside the round sees anything.
Legal basisArt. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
Retention7 days after the round ends (if a round never ends: 7 days after the last activity), after which the round and all related data are deleted.

Please note: situations may mention other people. Don't write anything that insults or humiliates others (see Terms of Use).

3.4 Selfie proof (photos)

Instead of asking a witness, you can mark a square with a selfie. This is optional.

DataThe photo you take with the front camera (live camera only, no access to your photo library), plus round, square, your nickname and time. Other people may be visible in the photo. The photo is resized on your device (longest side at most 1280 pixels, JPEG, at most 400 KB) and re-encoded; location and camera metadata (EXIF/GPS) are removed in the process before upload.
PurposeProof that the situation happened; shown in the round's feed and in the "Photos of the night" album.
Who sees it?Only the players in your round. Photos are not public. By default the shareable result image contains no photo; only if you choose it yourself is one of your own selfies added (see 3.9). Only round members can retrieve photos, via protected links that expire after 15 minutes. Other players could save a photo by taking a screenshot; we cannot technically prevent this.
ConsentBefore sending you confirm: "Everyone in the photo is okay with it." Please ask every person in the photo first. You must be in the photo yourself.
Legal basisFor you as the photographer: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (game feature you chose). For other people in the photo: their consent, which you obtain before sending, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and our legitimate interest in a working, safe game feature, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. [LAWYER REVIEW: legal basis for third parties; German right to one's own image (KUG); photos of teenagers under 16]
StorageStorage service of our provider Supabase (Supabase Storage). [REVIEW: same region Frankfurt am Main (eu-central-1) as the database]
RetentionWith the round (7 days after the round ends or after the last activity), then the photo and its image file are deleted.
Report & blockEvery photo can be reported – including with the reason "I'm in it and don't want it". A reported photo – whatever the reason – is deleted immediately, for everyone, including us; the mark stays. For review only the report remains (reason, situation, note) and who took the photo. If you block someone, you won't see their photos either.
No face recognitionPhotos are not analysed automatically, not used for face recognition or advertising and not passed on to third parties.

If you are in a photo: if you don't want to be in a selfie, tell the person who took it or report the photo in the app with the reason "I'm in it and don't want it" – it's gone for everyone right away. If you are not in the round, email us at apps@facenheel.de with the date and round name (you don't need the app for this). We delete the photo within 24 hours of finding it; after 7 days it is deleted anyway.

3.5 Reporting, removing and blocking

DataReports: who reported, what was reported (copy of the text or nickname, or reference to the photo), reason (e.g. offensive, sexual, personal data, "I'm in it and don't want it", other), optional note (up to 500 characters), time, whether we have handled it. The photo itself is not part of the report; it is deleted immediately (see 3.4). Bans: banned ID, optional reason, time. Squares removed by the host: which square, by whom, when. Blocks: who blocked whom, nickname, time.
PurposeProtection against inappropriate content, enforcing the Terms of Use, review of reports by us (daily; for new reports we receive a push notification that only states the number); excluding users who break the rules (a ban applies to creating, joining, writing, marking, witnessing and photos; photos of banned users are deleted immediately).
Legal basisArt. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a safe game environment and in complying with app store requirements).
RetentionReports: 30 days, independent of the round (if you delete your data, your report stays without your ID until then). Removals: with the round (7 days). Blocks: until you unblock or delete your data. Bans: until we lift them.

The blocked person is not told that they have been blocked. A word filter checks typed texts for gross insults; rejected texts are not saved.

3.6 Push notifications (optional)

DataYour device's push token (issued by Apple or Google via Expo), platform, app language, time of last update and of the last witness notification; content of notifications (e.g. a new round, pending witness requests or the reminder the day before a planned round with the round name and number of full cards, no nicknames).
PurposeNotifying you during a round about witness requests, the Hot Square and new rounds; for a round planned in advance, exactly one reminder the day before.
Legal basisYour consent via your operating system's notification permission, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Sec. 25(1) TDDDG. [LAWYER REVIEW: alternatively Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; consent of 13–15-year-olds, Art. 8 GDPR]
RetentionUntil you turn notifications off or delete your data. Tokens reported as invalid by Apple or Google (e.g. after uninstalling the app) are removed automatically, as are tokens not refreshed for 90 days.
WithdrawalAt any time in your device settings.

3.7 Camera

The app asks for camera access (a) to scan a round's QR code – the image is processed only on your device, neither stored nor transmitted – and (b) for selfie proof (see 3.4) – only the photo you actively send is uploaded. The app does not access your photo library. Access happens only with your permission in the operating system, which you can revoke at any time in your device settings. You can also type the round code instead. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, Sec. 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG.

3.8 Feedback in test versions

Only test versions (not the version from the App Store or Google Play) contain a feedback feature (thumbs up/down, optional text). It is stored without nicknames and deleted after 30 days. [REVIEW: store build is built without the feedback feature]

3.9 Stored on your device

The app stores on your device: session token, last used nickname, chosen language, your acceptance of the Terms of Use and which animations you have already seen. This data is deleted when you uninstall the app. A selfie is only kept briefly in the app's cache and deleted after sending or cancelling; nothing is saved to your photo library. Legal basis: Sec. 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Sharing the result image: the award-ceremony image (ranking and titles, without situation texts) is generated on your device. If you share it, this happens via your operating system's share sheet to recipients you choose; we receive nothing and publish nothing.

Your own selfie on the result image (optional): by default there is no photo on the image. With "Add my photo" you can add one of your own selfies – other people's photos and reported photos cannot be chosen. Before that you confirm: "Everyone in the photo is okay with it going public." Consent given when taking the photo only covers the round, not sharing. If you share the image, you are responsible for making sure everyone in it (and, for teenagers under 16, their parents) agrees. We cannot delete images once shared. [LAWYER REVIEW: responsibility when users share; consent of minors under KUG/GDPR]

7. Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), to object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) (Art. 21) and to withdraw consent with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

Easiest way: in the app under Settings → Your data → Delete my data you immediately delete your ID and the data linked to it. Since we don't know names or email addresses, we can only identify you in email requests if you tell us your user ID. You find it under Settings → Your data → Your ID ("Copy ID"). Write to apps@facenheel.de. More on the support page.

Right to lodge a complaint: you may complain to a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit), Alt-Moabit 59–61, 10555 Berlin, Germany.

8. Children and teenagers

Party-Bingo is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed specifically at children. We deliberately don't collect age or contact details.

If you are under 16, please ask your parents before allowing push notifications or using selfie proof. Don't take photos of children. [LAWYER REVIEW: consent of 13–15-year-olds under Art. 8 GDPR for push and photos; parental consent to the contract (Secs. 106 ff. BGB)]

If we learn that a child under 13 uses the app, we will delete the data when notified; parents can contact apps@facenheel.de.

9. Security

The connection between app and server is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Access rules (Row Level Security) ensure users only see data of their own rounds. Marks and the game feed cannot be read directly, only through checked server functions. Photo storage has its own rules: uploads only into your own folder of an active round and not while banned, reading only for round members without blocks, no later changes.
