for the “Party-Bingo” app · Last updated: 12 November 2026 · The German version is the reference text.

2. Summary

  • You need no account, no email address and no phone number. On first launch the app signs you in automatically with a random, anonymous ID.
  • We only process what the game needs: your nickname, the situations you type, the game history and – if you use selfie proof – your photos.
  • Rounds with all texts and photos are deleted automatically 7 days after the round ends (if a round never ends: 7 days after the last activity).
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics tools, no sale of data.
  • Data is stored by our provider Supabase on servers in Frankfurt am Main, Germany. Push notifications are sent via the Expo push service (USA).
  • The app uses the camera to scan QR codes and – only if you choose it – for selfie proof. Only your round sees your selfies – unless you share one yourself on the result image.

3. What data we process

3.1 Anonymous sign-in

DataRandom user ID (UUID), session token, time of sign-in; for the session our provider Supabase also stores the IP address and device type/app version (user agent) at sign-in and a security log of sign-in events (time, IP address). For connections see 3.10.
PurposeRecognising you in rounds without an account; preventing misuse.
Legal basisArt. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the app features you request).
RetentionAs long as you use the app. Via “Delete my data” (see 7) you delete all data linked to your ID; the ID itself disappears with its session data once its rounds are deleted. IDs that have not been used for 12 months are deleted automatically. We delete the sign-in security log together with the ID, at the latest after 12 months.

3.2 Nickname

DataThe nickname (2–20 characters) you enter when joining a round. You don't have to use your real name.
PurposeShown to the other players in the round.
Legal basisArt. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
RetentionWith the round (7 days). If you block someone, their nickname stays in your block list until you unblock them or delete your data; if the blocked person deletes their data, the entry disappears together with their ID (at the latest 8 days later).

3.3 Rounds, situations and game history

DataRound data (name, occasion, game mode, card size, end time, round code, host; for rounds planned in advance – at most 5 days – also the party day, optional time and time zone); your situations (text up to 120 characters, optional emoji, category); your bingo card; marks (square, time, witness or selfie, confirmed/rejected); your player colour; your number of jokers; in rounds with “Dare!”, the dares (task from our catalogue, who claimed “I did it!”, who completed it, who clapped for it, time, revoked by the host if applicable); game events, points, titles.
PurposeRunning the game: dealing cards, witness requests, selfie proof, dares, scoring and award ceremony.
Who sees it?All players in the same round see your nickname, the situations on their cards, your confirmed marks, your “I did it!” claim while a dare is running, the dares you completed (with the nickname of whoever clapped) and points. Anyone who has the round code sees only the round name, number of players and taken colours before joining. Otherwise nobody outside the round sees anything.
Legal basisArt. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
Retention7 days after the round ends (if a round never ends: 7 days after the last activity), after which the round and all related data are deleted. Your “I did it!” claim is deleted as soon as the dare is over (after 5 minutes at the latest) or you leave the round.

Please note: situations may mention other people. Don't write anything that insults or humiliates others (see Terms of Use).

3.4 Selfie proof (photos)

Instead of asking a witness, you can mark a square with a selfie. This is optional.

DataThe photo you take with the front camera (live camera only, no access to your photo library), plus round, square, your nickname and time. Other people may be visible in the photo. The photo is resized on your device (longest side at most 1280 pixels, JPEG, at most 400 KB) and re-encoded; location and camera metadata (EXIF/GPS) are removed in the process before upload.
PurposeProof that the situation happened; shown in the round's feed and in the “Photos of the night” album.
Who sees it?Only the players in your round. Photos are not public. By default the shareable result image contains no photo; only if you choose it yourself is one of your own selfies added (see 3.9). Only round members can retrieve photos, via protected links that expire after 15 minutes. Other players could save a photo by taking a screenshot; we cannot technically prevent this.
ConsentBefore sending you confirm: “Everyone in the photo is okay with it.” Please ask every person in the photo first. You must be in the photo yourself.
Legal basisFor you as the photographer: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (game feature you chose). For other people in the photo: their consent, which you obtain before sending, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and our legitimate interest in a working, safe game feature, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
StorageStorage service of our provider Supabase (Supabase Storage). Same region as the database: Frankfurt am Main (eu-central-1).
RetentionWith the round (7 days after the round ends or after the last activity), then the photo and its image file are deleted.
Report & blockEvery photo can be reported – including with the reason “I'm in it and don't want it”. A reported photo – whatever the reason – immediately becomes invisible to everyone, including us; the image file is deleted in the process (technically within seconds, at the latest with the next clean-up run). The mark stays. Only the report (reason, situation, note) and the name of whoever took the photo are kept for review. If you block someone, you won't see their photos either.
No face recognitionPhotos are not analysed automatically, not used for face recognition or advertising and not passed on to third parties.

If you are in a photo: if you don't want to be in a selfie, tell the person who took it or report the photo in the app with the reason “I'm in it and don't want it” – it's gone for everyone right away. If you are not in the round, email us at apps@facenheel.de with the date and round name or code (you don't need the app for this). We delete the photo within 24 hours of finding it; after 7 days it is deleted anyway.

3.5 Reporting, removing and blocking

DataReports: who reported, who or what was reported (ID of the reported person, copy of the text or nickname, or reference to the photo), round, reason (e.g. offensive, sexual, personal data, “I'm in it and don't want it”, other), optional note (up to 500 characters), time, whether we have handled it. The photo itself is not part of the report; it is removed immediately (see 3.4). Bans: banned ID, optional reason, time. Squares removed by the host: which square, by whom, when. Blocks: who blocked whom, nickname, time.
PurposeProtection against inappropriate content, enforcing the Terms of Use, review of reports by us (daily; for new reports we receive a push notification that only states the number); excluding users who break the rules (a ban applies to creating, joining, writing, marking, witnessing and photos; photos of banned users are removed immediately).
Legal basisArt. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a safe game environment and in complying with app store requirements).
RetentionReports: 30 days, independent of the round (if you delete your data, your report stays without your ID until then). Removals: with the round (7 days). Blocks: until you unblock or delete your data. Bans: until we lift them.

The blocked person is not told that they have been blocked. A word filter checks typed texts for gross insults; rejected texts are not saved.

3.6 Push notifications (optional)

DataYour device's push token (the app obtains it from the Expo push service, which receives your Apple or Google device token for this), platform, app language, time of last update and of the last witness notification and whether the day-before reminder has already been sent; content of notifications (e.g. hot time, a new dare or the reminder the day before a planned round with the round name and number of full cards – each without nicknames). Notifications about witness requests and confirmations show the other player's nickname and the situation; notifications that other players claimed “I did it!” or that the host started a new round show their nicknames.
PurposeNotifying you during a round about witness requests, hot time, dares (“Dare!”) and new rounds; for a round planned in advance, exactly one reminder the day before.
Legal basisYour consent via your operating system's notification permission, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Sec. 25(1) TDDDG.
RetentionUntil you turn notifications off or delete your data. Tokens reported as invalid by Apple or Google (e.g. after uninstalling the app) are removed automatically; all tokens at the latest once they have not been refreshed for 90 days.
WithdrawalAt any time in your device settings.

3.7 Camera

The app asks for camera access (a) to scan a round's QR code – the image is processed only on your device, neither stored nor transmitted – and (b) for selfie proof (see 3.4) – only the photo you actively send is uploaded. The app does not access your photo library. Access happens only with your permission in the operating system, which you can revoke at any time in your device settings. You can also type the round code instead. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, Sec. 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG.

3.8 Feedback in test versions

Only test versions (not the version from the App Store or Google Play) contain a feedback feature (thumbs up/down, optional text up to 500 characters). Along with it we store the round's occasion, game mode, card size and number of players plus a hash of round and ID so that resending replaces the old entry – without nicknames. Deleted after 30 days.

3.9 Stored on your device

The app stores on your device: session token, last used nickname and last chosen player colour, chosen language, the “vibration” switch, your acceptance of the Terms of Use, whether we have already asked you about push notifications, which hints and animations you have already seen, which results and banners you have hidden, cached suggestion packs and the address of our server. This data is deleted when you uninstall the app or use “Delete my data” (the server address remains; it contains no data about you). A selfie is only kept briefly in the app's cache and deleted after sending or cancelling; nothing is saved to your photo library. Legal basis: Sec. 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Sharing the result image: the award-ceremony image (ranking and titles, without situation texts) is generated on your device. If you share it, this happens via your operating system's share sheet to recipients you choose; we receive nothing and publish nothing.

Your own selfie on the result image (optional): by default there is no photo on the image. With “Add my photo” you can add one of your own selfies – other people's photos and reported photos cannot be chosen. Before that you confirm: “Everyone in the photo is okay with it going public.” Consent given when taking the photo only covers the round, not sharing. If you share the image, you are responsible for making sure everyone in it (and, for teenagers under 16, their parents) agrees. We cannot delete images once shared.

3.10 Server logs

When you access our servers, Supabase processes technically necessary connection data (IP address, time, endpoint, device type/app version) in logs. Purpose: operation, security, debugging. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Retention: as long as our provider technically requires, currently no more than 7 days.

5. Who receives your data

We don't sell data or pass it on for advertising. We use the following service providers:

Supabase (database, sign-in, server functions, photo storage): Supabase Inc., USA. Stored in a data centre in Frankfurt am Main, Germany (region eu-central-1, operated on Amazon Web Services). A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with Supabase (Supabase Data Processing Addendum). Access from third countries (e.g. the USA) for support and maintenance cannot be ruled out; safeguarded by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

Expo (sending push notifications): 650 Industries, Inc. (Expo), USA. Push token and notification content are sent to the Expo push service, which forwards them to Apple or Google. This involves a transfer to the USA. Basis: data processing agreement with Expo under Art. 28 GDPR; the transfer is safeguarded by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). Photos are not sent to Expo.

GitHub (app configuration file): GitHub, Inc., USA. When it starts, the app loads a small file from our website on GitHub Pages that contains only the address of our server and its public access key. GitHub processes technically necessary connection data such as your device's IP address in server logs to deliver the file and protect it against attacks (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). This may involve a transfer to the USA. GitHub receives no game data (ID, nickname, texts, photos).

Apple or Google deliver push notifications. The respective app store's privacy policy applies to downloading the app.

Other players in the same round see the content described in 3.3 and 3.4. Otherwise we only disclose data where legally required.

6. No ads, no tracking, no automated decisions

Party-Bingo contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools and no advertising IDs. There is no profiling and no automated decision-making within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR. The word filter merely rejects individual texts.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), to object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) (Art. 21) and to withdraw consent with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

Easiest way: in the app under Settings → Your data → Delete my data you immediately delete your nicknames, texts, photos, block list and push token. Your marks stay without name or text so that the other players' points stay correct; your ID disappears once the rounds you were in are deleted. Since we don't know names or email addresses, we can only identify you in email requests if you tell us your user ID. You find it under Settings → Your data → Your ID (“Copy ID”). Write to apps@facenheel.de. More on the support page.

Right to lodge a complaint: you may complain to a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit), Alt-Moabit 59–61, 10555 Berlin, Germany.

8. Children and teenagers

Party-Bingo is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed specifically at children. We deliberately don't collect age or contact details.

If you are under 16, please ask your parents before allowing push notifications or using selfie proof. Don't take photos of children.

If we learn that a child under 13 uses the app, we will delete the data when notified; parents can contact apps@facenheel.de.

9. Security

The connection between app and server is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Access rules (Row Level Security) ensure users only see data of their own rounds. Marks and the game feed cannot be read directly, only through checked server functions. Photo storage has its own rules: uploads only into your own folder of an active round and not while banned, reading only for round members without blocks, no later changes.

10. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in server logs to deliver the site and protect it against attacks (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). This may involve a transfer to the USA. The website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from third-party servers (not even fonts).
