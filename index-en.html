Bingo for every party

The same things happen at every party. Someone tells the story everyone knows. Someone dances first. Party-Bingo turns it into a game.

  • Free
  • No account
  • No ads
  • No tracking
  • 13+
  • English & German

How it works

  • One person starts a round, the others join via QR code or code – just with a nickname.
  • Everyone types situations that might happen tonight.
  • Something on your card happened? Snap a selfie with it or have a friend confirm.
  • Line complete? BINGO!

Rounds, texts and photos are deleted after 7 days. Party-Bingo is not a drinking game.
