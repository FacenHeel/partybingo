FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. The app signs you in automatically with an anonymous ID. You only enter a nickname per round.

How do I join a round?

Scan the QR code on the host's phone or type the round code.

How does selfie proof work?

Tap the square, choose "📸 Selfie proof", take a selfie with the situation – you must be in it and everyone in the photo must be okay with it. Only your round sees the photo; it is deleted with the round after 7 days.

I don't get notifications.

Check in your device settings that notifications are allowed for Party-Bingo.

How long are my texts and photos stored?

Rounds with all texts and photos are deleted 7 days after the round ends (rounds never ended: 7 days after the last activity). Details in the privacy policy.

Report something unpleasant

In the app you can report any text, nickname or photo and block players. Reported photos are deleted immediately. If you're in a photo and don't want to be, choose "I'm in it and don't want it". We check reports daily.

If you appear in a photo without playing, or it's urgent, email apps@facenheel.de with the date, round name or code and a short description. You don't need the app for this.

Child safety

Party-Bingo is for ages 13 and up. We have zero tolerance for any content that sexualises or exploits children. We remove such content immediately, permanently exclude the users and report them to the competent authorities. Delete my data

In the app: Settings → Your data → Delete my data → confirm. This immediately deletes your nicknames, texts, photos, block list and push token. Your marks stay without name or text so that your fellow players' points stay correct. Your anonymous ID disappears once the rounds you were in are deleted.

By email: write to apps@facenheel.de with the subject "Delete my data" and your user ID from the app (Settings → Your data → Your ID → "Copy ID"). We delete your data within 7 days and confirm by email. If you are in a photo and don't have the app, the date and round name or code are enough.

Deleted automatically: rounds with texts and photos 7 days after the round ends (or after the last activity), IDs after 12 months without use. Uninstalling the app removes the data on your device.

May be kept longer: reports up to 30 days for handling (without your ID); server logs [REVIEW: duration per Supabase plan, e.g. 1 day (Free) or 7 days (Pro)].
