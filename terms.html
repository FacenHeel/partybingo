1. What this is about

Party-Bingo is a free party game. You type situations that might happen at a party and cross them off when another player witnesses them or you take a selfie with them. If the host switches on “Dare!”, harmless dares for everyone pop up now and then. Before you send your own texts or photos, you accept these terms in the app. The app is intended for people aged 13 and over. If you are a minor, please talk to your parents about using it.

The respective app store's standard licence agreement also applies (for Apple: the “Licensed Application End User License Agreement”).

Friends' names in situations are fine as long as it stays friendly (“Lisa starts dancing” yes, anything degrading no).

“Dare!”: In rounds where the host has switched on “Dare!”, the app now and then suggests a short, harmless task for everyone (e.g. an Oscar-style thank-you speech). The tasks come from our fixed catalogue and contain no encouragement to drink alcohol and nothing dangerous. Nobody has to take part. Don't do anything that puts you or others at risk or embarrasses them, and ask others before involving them. Only tap “I did it!” if you really did it.

3. Special rules for selfie proof

  • You must be in the photo yourself.
  • Everyone in the photo must agree. Ask first. Anyone who doesn't want to be in it stays out.
  • No photos of children, no photos of people in embarrassing or intimate situations, no photos in toilets, changing rooms or similar places.
  • Photos are meant for your round only. Don't save or share other people's photos without their consent.
  • Result image: you may only add one of your own selfies to the result image, and only if everyone in it agrees to it going public – for teenagers under 16, their parents too. You are responsible for sharing it.

4. Report, remove, block

  • Report: any text, nickname or photo of another player can be reported. Reported squares are replaced for you immediately; reported photos immediately become invisible to everyone and are deleted.
  • Remove: the host of a round can remove a square or a mark for everyone.
  • Block: you can block other players. Their texts and photos are hidden from you and you can no longer ask each other to be witnesses. Blocks also apply to future rounds.
  • Word filter: gross insults are rejected automatically.
  • Our response: we check reports daily, at the latest within 24 hours, and remove unacceptable content. Users who break these rules are permanently excluded.
